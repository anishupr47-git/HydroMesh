# HydroMesh Safety Policy & Deterministic Validator (R1–R11)

> **CORE INVARIANT: Fail Closed.**
> The safety validator is pure, deterministic, performs zero I/O, and calls zero LLMs. If any check fails, or if confidence drops below threshold, execution halts or falls back to human operator review. Advisory actions never auto-actuate physical hardware.

---

## The R1–R11 Deterministic Safety Matrix

| Rule | Check | Trigger Condition | Deterministic Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **R1** | Confidence Floor | Decision confidence $< 0.80$ | `needs_human` |
| **R2** | Entity Resolution | Unknown gate ID, desander basin ID, or feeder key | `rejected` |
| **R3** | Flow Shock Clamp | Proposed flow reduction $> 50\%$ of current flow | Clamp to max 50% drop + record audit note |
| **R4** | Baseload Floor | Proposed target flow $< 20\%$ of plant design flow | Clamp up to 20% floor + record audit note |
| **R5** | Structural Capacity | Proposed target flow $>$ plant design flow capacity | `rejected` |
| **R6** | Sluice Flush Window | Flush duration outside $[5, 90]$ minutes | `rejected` |
| **R7** | Desander Cooldown | Basin currently flushing or last flush $< 60$ minutes ago | `rejected` |
| **R8** | Action Throttling | $> 3$ state actions executed in last 10 minutes | `rejected` |
| **R9** | Generation Ceiling | Target generation MW $>$ available hydrological MW capacity | `rejected` |
| **R10**| Feeder Priority | Critical auxiliary/hospital feeders not first in dispatch sequence | `rejected` |
| **R11**| Null Action Check | Proposed action list is completely empty | `needs_human` |

If ANY action in a proposed plan receives `rejected`, the entire plan's `SafetyVerdict` evaluates to `rejected`.

---

## Fail-Closed Principle

1. **Pure Function**: The safety evaluation `validate(decision, twin_snapshot)` depends solely on the immutable inputs provided. No database queries, no external network calls, no asynchronous race conditions.
2. **Deterministic Clamping**: Rules R3 and R4 clamp flow modulation parameters to hydraulically safe envelopes rather than failing outright, protecting penstock pipes from water hammer transients.
3. **No LLM in the Loop**: Language models propose candidate decisions; the validator evaluates them against hardcoded mathematical and physical invariants. An LLM cannot override or disable a rule.

---

## Advisory-Only Operations & Dispatch Guardrails

All proposed actions generated by HydroMesh (`ModulateIntake`, `TriggerFlush`, `ReallocateDispatch`) are strictly advisory recommendations presented to the station control room:
- **No Direct Actuation**: HydroMesh does not connect directly to PLC relays, governor solenoids, or SCADA actuator write-coils.
- **Operator Override Window**: Any high-severity recommendation carries a configurable 600-second (10-minute) countdown window. Control room operators can reject, modify, or override the plan at any moment.

---

## What HydroMesh Will NEVER Do

1. **NEVER auto-actuate valves, gates, or breakers** without verified human operator confirmation.
2. **NEVER bypass or relax the safety validator**, regardless of LLM confidence scores or urgency prompts.
3. **NEVER act on unvalidated or unparsed LLM output**.
4. **NEVER silence critical sediment alarms** or exceed structural penstock limits.
5. **NEVER drop environmental baseload river flow** below the 20% regulatory ecological requirement.
6. **NEVER transmit proprietary plant coordinates without coarsening** (0.1° grid buffer on global aggregate maps).
7. **NEVER accept re-dispatches with missing or duplicate cryptographic nonces** (replay attack mitigation).
