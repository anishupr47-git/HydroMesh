# High Sediment Influx Response — SOP-HY-001

## 1. Trigger Conditions
Activate when:
- `silt_ppm >= 1,200` PPM at intake ultrasonic sensor, OR
- `turbidity >= 800` NTU at desander basin entry, OR
- Predictive forecast indicates incoming sediment surge exceeding $2,000\,\text{PPM}$ within 6 hours.

## 2. Immediate Operational Protocol
1. **Desander Gate Verification**: Confirm desander flushing sluice gate actuators are online and unblocked.
2. **Sequential Flush Initiation**:
   - Begin with Basin #1 sluice opening at 15% travel rate.
   - Maintain minimum flush duration of 30 minutes, not exceeding 90 minutes.
   - Enforce minimum 60-minute cooldown before subsequent flush cycles on the same basin.
3. **Power Output Coordination**:
   - Reallocate dispatch load across remaining active units.
   - Ensure baseload intake does not drop below 20% of design capacity to safeguard downstream ecological flow.

## 3. High Silt (> 3,000 PPM) Escalation
If silt levels exceed $3,000\,\text{PPM}$:
- Sound audible control room alarm.
- Prepare station for controlled turbine shutdown if silt remains elevated for more than 45 consecutive minutes.
- Record ultrasonic runner thickness baseline prior to restart.
