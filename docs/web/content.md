# HydroMesh Web Content & Editorial Guidelines

> Standard copy, claims verification rules, and mandatory honesty disclaimers.

---

## 1. Mandatory Honesty Disclaimers

Every public page and documentation surface must display the following disclaimer in prominent placement:

> **Notice**: HydroMesh operates in demonstration mode against a stylized digital twin hydrological simulator. All impact metrics, operational cost savings, and equipment lifetimes are simulated figures. Advisory recommendations require human operator authorization and never auto-actuate physical plant systems.

---

## 2. Core Value Proposition Copy

- **Headline**: Spatio-Temporal Intelligence for Himalayan Hydropower.
- **Subheadline**: Forecast sediment surges 12–24 hours ahead, couple real-time river discharge to IEC 62364 turbine wear physics, and eliminate unexpected monsoon overhaul shutdowns.
- **Problem Statement**: Steep Himalayan rivers carry ultra-hard quartz sediment that erodes turbine runners in weeks, costing operators up to NPR 80 Lakhs every monsoon season in emergency repairs and lost generation.
- **Solution**: HydroMesh combines gradient boosting triage, physics-informed forecasting, and deterministic safety validation to recommend desander flushes and intake throttling before catastrophic wear occurs.

---

## 3. Claims & Metrics Policy

1. **Monetary Savings**: Quote as *"Simulated savings of up to NPR 80 Lakhs per monsoon season based on runner overhaul avoidance"*. Never present as empirical historical savings without benchmark qualifiers.
2. **Alert Windows**: Always specify the **10-minute operator acknowledgement window** for RED alerts.
3. **Safety Rules**: Highlight that all agent proposals must clear **Rules R1 through R11** before being surfaced to operators.
